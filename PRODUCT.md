## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS v4 (CSS-first `@theme`), `motion`, `@number-flow/react`, `clsx`. Set by the client brief.

## Users

Algerian business owners (clinics, schools, shops, labs, real-estate agencies, wholesalers) comparing what a web, e-commerce, SaaS or AI project costs before writing to an agency. They read on a phone, often from an Instagram link, and decide in WhatsApp.

## Product Purpose

The pricing page of Mindak Creative Agency© (El Achour, Algiers), part of the Mindak group with Mindak Creative Studio©. It shows four categories (Sites vitrine, E-commerce, Logiciels SaaS, Intelligence artificielle), fixed starting prices in DA, what each pack includes, options, and a comparison. Success = a visitor opens WhatsApp with a prefilled message naming the pack.

## Positioning

A creative studio that shoots podcasts and brand content also builds the software. The same print-like editorial care seen on the @mindak.studio grid applies to the sites it sells. Prices are public and fixed "à partir de".

## Voice

French first. Confident, short sentences, studio voice. No filler adjectives ("haute performance", "révolutionnaire"), no emoji. One optional Darja line.

## Constraints

- Prices and features are fixed by the client and must not change.
- No stock photos, no testimonials, no client logos, no invented proof. ROI examples are labelled "Exemples indicatifs, non garantis."
- Contact is WhatsApp only (`NEXT_PUBLIC_WHATSAPP`, format 213XXXXXXXXX) plus Instagram.
- Lighthouse mobile ≥ 95 on all four categories; AA contrast; usable at 360px.
